Zepto Delivery Policy and FAQs

1. Delivery Time Guarantees
Zepto is committed to delivering your groceries in 10 minutes under normal conditions. However, delivery times may vary based on weather conditions, traffic, and distance from the store. If a delivery takes more than 20 minutes, customers are eligible for a 10% discount on their next order. 

2. Cancellation Policy
You can cancel your order within 60 seconds of placing it without any cancellation fee. If you cancel after 60 seconds, a cancellation fee of Rs. 50 will be charged. If the delivery partner has already reached your location, the order cannot be canceled and you will be charged the full amount.

3. Refund Policy
If you receive damaged or expired products, you must report it within 24 hours of delivery. Our support team will verify the claim and initiate a full refund for the affected items within 3-5 business days. We do not offer exchanges for perishable goods.

4. Premium Membership (Zepto Pass)
Zepto Pass members enjoy free delivery on all orders above Rs. 99. The membership costs Rs. 299 per month. Premium members also get priority support and early access to discounts.

5. Operating Hours
We operate from 6:00 AM to 1:00 AM, seven days a week. Orders placed outside these hours will be scheduled for the next day's first delivery slot.
